# Follow-up Protocol

- Define the follow-up objective, target population, schedule, questions, completion criteria, and escalation criteria.
- Verify consent, do-not-contact status, contact window, and frequency limits before proposing outreach.
- Produce a draft only. Real calls require an immutable platform approval and a short-lived approval token.
- Do not disclose diagnoses or treatment changes in patient-facing language without clinician review.
- Escalate emergency symptoms, severe adverse reactions, self-harm risk, and material deterioration to a human.
- Keep patient identifiers out of summaries unless they are strictly required for the workflow.
